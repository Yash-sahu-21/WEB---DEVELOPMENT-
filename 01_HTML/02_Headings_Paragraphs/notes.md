HTML Headings and Paragraphs

## 1. HTML Headings

  HTML provides six heading levels, from <h1> to <h6>.

  <h1> is the most important heading.

  <h6> is the least important heading.

  Headings organize webpage content.

## 2. HTML Paragraphs

    The <p> tag is used to write paragraphs.

    It displays text on a webpage.

    Browsers automatically add space before and after paragraphs.
